The LumaRim experience

Lumina is a handcrafted Skyrim experience focused on survival, immersion, and meaningful progression. Built around modern graphics with Community Shaders, challenging gameplay, and extensive optimization, it delivers a stable and cohesive world, every mod chosen and tuned by hand to work as one.

Gameplay

Built on Simonrim, rebuilt as our own

Lumina starts from the Simonrim base, then changes and alters it into something of our own. A custom difficulty overhaul inspired by Requiem, our own death alternative, and everything rebuilt and redone on SKSE so it runs clean with almost no script lag. We call it LumaRim.

Modern Standards

Modern graphics with Community Shaders

Built around modern graphics with Community Shaders and custom mods made in-house. Not a pile of presets, but a cohesive visual identity crafted for beauty and performance alike.
