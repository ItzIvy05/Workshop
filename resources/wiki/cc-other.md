# Other Creations

## Bone Wolf

Bone Wolf is mostly stock, with these changes:

- Bone Wolves have smaller heads.
- Bone Wolves are no longer level scaled from 5 to 30. They are level 8, and the Zombie/Bone Wolf leveled list has been updated to reflect this.
- Bone Wolves have a chance to drop Mort Flesh in addition to their usual Bone Meal.

## Plague of the Dead

Plague of the Dead is mostly stock. The one change:

- The zombie night ambushes are disabled, so zombies will no longer jump you at night.
