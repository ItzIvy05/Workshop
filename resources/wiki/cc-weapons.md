# Weapons

## CC Staves

Handled by **CC Staves - Mysticism Consistency Patch**, which keeps the Creation Club staves consistent with Mysticism, Lumina's magic overhaul.

## Elite Crossbows and Expanded Crossbow Pack

Handled by multiple mods, plus my own changes yoinked from Requiem SkyGround.

- High-level Vigilants of Stendarr carry either Steel or Silver Crossbows.
- You may sometimes find Silver Crossbows carried by the Silver Hand.
- You may sometimes find Imperial Crossbows carried by Imperial Soldiers.
- You may sometimes find Orcish Crossbows carried by Stronghold Orcs.
- Dwarven Crossbows can rarely be found in Dwarven dungeons.
- The Fletcher in Solitude occasionally sells military surplus crossbows.
- Crossbow stats are rebalanced for consistency with Kachuck.

## Fangs of Solitude - Dawnfang and Duskfang Reintegration

A reintegration of the Dawnfang and Duskfang Creation that moves the vault to the Solitude Sewers, as found in Skyrim Extended Cut - Saints and Seducers. Check the [mod page](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/184501) for full details.

- The vault is now the **Solitude Sewers Vault**, connected to the Solitude Sewers. It is no longer in Riften by the Ratway.
- **Bloodthirst** is at Bloodlet Throne. Read the note in the vault to start that objective.

## Stendarr's Hammer

Handled by **Stendarr's Chosen - Stendarr's Hammer Reintegration**. Check the [mod page](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/110617) for full details.

- The hammer is no longer in Calcelmo's museum.
- Once you are level 15 or higher, the next town, city or settlement you enter picks an impact site for it. The site is not always the same.
- The first time you approach that site, Stendarr's Hammer falls from the sky and leaves a crater.
- You have to be deemed worthy by Stendarr to pick it up.

## Sunder & Wraithguard

Fully removed from Lumina and replaced by a proper quest mod, **The Tools of Kagrenac**, running with my own **The Tools of Kagrenac - Unofficial Patch**. Check the [mod page](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/14168) and the [patch page](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/187672) for full details.

- The quest starts a couple of days after you finish Arniel's Endeavor and claim Keening, when a courier brings you a letter. Keep Keening in your inventory.
- Your progress is checked every time you travel, so the quest will not silently fail to start.
- The MCM has a **Minimum Player Level** (1 to 80) so the quest does not start before you are ready, and a **Status** page showing which requirements you have met.
- Turn on **Wraithguard's Aegis** in the MCM to stay protected from Keening and Sunder's mortal wound without wearing Wraithguard.
- There is no alternate start.

## Umbra

Handled by **Creation Club Umbra - Reduced Cut**. Check the [mod page](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/170664) for full details.

- The Umbra quest, its dungeons and all the items it added to the world are removed.
- The sword itself is in the Soul Cairn, at the Soul Cairn Dragon Lair.
