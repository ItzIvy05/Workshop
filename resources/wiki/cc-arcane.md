# Arcane Archer & Accessories

## Arcane Accessories

Handled by **Arcane Accessories - Tweaks and Enhancements**. Check the [mod page](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/118318) for full details.

### Getting the spells

- There is no spell chest in Hob's Fall Cave.
- Spell tomes, staves, scrolls and robes show up as random loot in Apocrypha, and the Dremora Merchant from the matching Black Book sells them.
- A spell tome only shows up once your skill in that school is high enough. Master tomes also need that school's Ritual quest done. Staves and scrolls have no such limit.

### Spells

- Every spell is Master level, except the Elemental and Absorb spells, which moved up one tier.
- Spell costs are higher, so they do not make vanilla spells obsolete.
- Every spell has a staff. Every spell except the Concentration ones has a scroll.
- The Unbounded spells are now **Unbounded Inferno**, **Unbounded Blizzard** and **Unbounded Thunderstorm**. They deal fire damage, with a chance to also deal frost and shock damage.
- Mara's Wrath is now **Meridia's Retribution**. It deals fire damage plus extra sun damage to undead, and its Turn Undead effect works with perks that affect Turn Undead.
- **Pride of Hirstaang** summons 2 stronger spirit bears.
- **Fenrik's Welcome** opens locks up to Adept, not Expert.

## Arcane Archer

Handled by **Arcane Archer - Tweaks and Enhancements**. Check the [mod page](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/95716) for full details.

### Arrows

- **Refined Dragonbone Arrows** deal 28 damage. Craft them from 1 Dragon Bone and 24 Dragonbone Arrows.
- **Soul Stealer, Explosive and Telekinesis Arrows** deal 16 damage, count as Elven, and need a Moonstone Ingot to craft.
- **Explosive Arrows** take only 1 Elemental Salt, but need the Elven Smithing and Arcane Blacksmith perks.
- **Bound Arrows** deal 25 damage.
- Casting **Bound Quiver** gives Conjuration experience.

### Telekinesis Arrows

- They use everything a normal arrow does: archery perks, bow damage, tempering, enchantments, sneak attack damage and poisons.
- All of it is applied **when you use the lesser power to launch them**. Poison or enchant your bow first, then launch, and every arrow in the volley gets it.
- You get **one hovering arrow per 10 levels of Alteration**: 3 at level 30, 5 at level 50, 10 at level 100. Never fewer than 1, never more than 10.
