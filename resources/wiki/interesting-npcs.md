# Interesting NPCs

## Recruiting

Not everyone will follow you just because you ask. Depending on who they are, you may need to reach a certain level, build the right skills, join the right side, or simply be the right sort of person.

If someone turns you down, come back later. You are usually missing something rather than being refused outright.

## Zora Fair-Child

A Breton two-handed fighter who charges in first and asks questions later, though she handles a bow or a spell well enough when it suits her.

**Where to find her:** Brittleshin Pass.

**Recruiting her:** She joins freely. There are no requirements to meet first.

### The Children Fair

Zora's follower quest. To start it, tell her to meet you in Riverwood and then dismiss her. She greets you when you next enter the Sleeping Giant Inn.
